# paynest-grc-project
Simulated fintech GRC project: IT Act 2000, CERT-In Directions and DPDPA 2023 mapped to ISO 27001:2022 with gap scoring and remediation roadmap.

Self-initiated project. PayNest is a fictional company; no real client or data is involved.

## What it shows
A requirements register for a simulated 52-person Mumbai payments startup, mapping the IT Act 2000, SPDI Rules 2011, CERT-In Directions 2022 and DPDPA 2023 / Rules 2025 to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A, with maturity and risk scoring, remediation, owners and priorities.

## Contents
- 01-scenario: company profile and current security posture
- 02-requirements-register: 35 requirements, gap scoring, 30/60/90-day roadmap

## Notes
- Legal summaries are in plain English and should be checked against official texts.
- ISO 27001 has limited privacy coverage; privacy rows are marked partial and refer to ISO/IEC 27701.
